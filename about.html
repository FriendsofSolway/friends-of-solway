👋 About Us

Kia ora! We're Friends of Solway, the parent-led fundraising committee for Solway Primary School in Masterton.

Who we are

Parents and caregivers, giving back

We're a group of parents and caregivers who volunteer our time to support and improve Solway Primary School for the benefit of every child in our community.

We work alongside the school to raise money for the extras that make school life richer for our tamariki: things that sit outside the school's day-to-day budget. Every dollar we raise goes straight back into Solway.
